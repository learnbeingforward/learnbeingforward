1.Purpose and scope

The purpose of this MOU is to set out the terms on which the Company shall deliver the training programme described below to the identified students of the College, and the obligations of each Party in respect of scheduling, conduct, attendance, assessment and payment.

3.Sections and topics to be covered

  1. Core Java — OOP, collections and exception handling
  2. Data structures — arrays, strings, linked lists, stacks and queues
  3. Algorithms — searching, sorting, recursion and two pointers
  4. Trees, graphs and dynamic programming basics
  5. Placement coding practice and mock assessments

4.Schedule and timings

  1. Once this MOU is signed and the training dates are confirmed, both Parties shall strictly adhere to the confirmed dates. The training shall run for 10 days from 01 Dec 2026 to 12 Dec 2026.
  2. Each batch shall be trained for 6 hours per day between 9:30 AM and 4:30 PM. Session start and end times shall be followed as published in the schedule shared on the portal.
  3. The College shall ensure that students are released from other academic activities during the scheduled hours and that the classroom or lab is available and ready at least 15 minutes before the first session of each day.
  4. Breaks within a training day shall be as per the College timetable and shall not reduce the agreed training hours.

5.Postponement, cancellation and delay charges

  1. No postponement after confirmation. Once the dates are confirmed, the training shall not be postponed or rescheduled by either Party, except in the circumstances described in the clause on force majeure.
  2. If a postponement is unavoidable, the Party seeking it shall give at least 7 days’ written notice to the other Party, along with the reason and proposed new dates.
  3. Delay charges. If any scheduled training day is postponed, cancelled or not delivered, the Party responsible for the postponement shall bear delay charges of ₹5,000 per affected training day, in addition to any direct costs already incurred by the other Party (such as trainer travel and accommodation).
  4. Where the postponement is caused by the College (for example, examinations, events, holidays or non-availability of students or rooms that were not communicated before confirmation), the delay charges shall be added to the College’s invoice.
  5. Where the postponement is caused by the Company (for example, non-availability of the trainer), the Company shall arrange a replacement trainer of equivalent expertise or deliver the missed hours at no extra cost, and the delay charges shall be adjusted against the College’s invoice.
  6. Missed hours shall be rescheduled by mutual agreement within the training period wherever possible.

6.Responsibilities of the Company

  1. Provide qualified trainers for the agreed course and sections, and replace a trainer promptly if required.
  2. Share the day-wise plan, session schedule and course content through the Learn Being Forward portal.
  3. Ensure that trainers report on time, follow the published timings, maintain professional conduct and dress code, and mark attendance for every session on the portal.
  4. Conduct assessments where included in the programme and share results with the College through the portal.
  5. Issue certificates to eligible students as per the clause on certificates.
  6. Keep student data confidential and use it only for delivering and reporting on the training.

7.Responsibilities of the College

  1. Provide a classroom or computer lab for each batch with working systems (for technical training), a projector or display with HDMI connectivity, power supply, a whiteboard and a stable internet connection.
  2. Nominate a single point of contact (training / placement coordinator) who is available during training hours.
  3. Ensure the attendance and discipline of students, and support the trainer in maintaining classroom conduct.
  4. Share the final list of students for each batch, with branch and semester, at least 3 working days before the start date, and register the students on the portal.
  5. Review and approve attendance and other requests raised on the portal within a reasonable time.
  6. Not engage the trainers deputed by the Company directly or through any other agency, for training of a similar nature, during the term of this MOU and for 12 months thereafter.

8.Certificates

A certificate of completion shall be issued to every student with at least 75% attendance. The College may request, in writing on the portal, that a certificate be issued to a student below this threshold for a genuine reason; the Company may approve such a request at its discretion.

10.Payment terms

  1. The College shall clear the full invoice amount within 20 working days of the completion of the training, by bank transfer to the account mentioned on the invoice.
  2. Late payment. If the payment is not received within 20 working days of the training completion, the College shall be liable to pay interest at 15% per day on the outstanding amount, from the day after the due date until the date of actual payment.
  3. Tax deducted at source, if any, shall be deducted as per law, and the College shall issue the TDS certificate within the statutory time.
  4. Once the Company’s accounts team marks the payment as received, a paid invoice shall be generated and shared with the College on the portal.
  5. The Company reserves the right to withhold certificates, assessment reports and further training under any other engagement until the outstanding amount, including interest, is cleared.

11.Confidentiality

Each Party shall keep confidential the commercial terms of this MOU, the course content and materials of the Company, and the personal data of students, and shall not disclose them to any third party without the prior written consent of the other Party, except as required by law. The College shall not discuss or negotiate commercial terms with the trainers directly; all such matters shall be addressed to the Company.

12.Intellectual property

All course content, presentations, assessments, notes and other materials provided by the Company remain the intellectual property of the Company. They are provided for the use of the enrolled students only and shall not be copied, recorded, distributed or used for any other training without the Company’s written consent.

13.Term and termination

  1. This MOU comes into force on the date of signing by both Parties and remains valid until the completion of the training and the settlement of all payments.
  2. Either Party may terminate this MOU by giving 15 days’ written notice if the other Party materially breaches its terms and does not remedy the breach within 7 days of being notified.
  3. On termination, the College shall pay for all training days delivered up to the date of termination, together with any delay charges and interest due.

14.Additional terms

None.

15.Force majeure

Neither Party shall be liable for a delay or failure caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including natural disasters, epidemics, government orders, riots or prolonged disruption of essential services. The affected Party shall inform the other promptly, and the Parties shall agree new dates for the affected days. Delay charges under clause 5 shall not apply to such events.

16.Dispute resolution

The Parties shall first attempt to resolve any dispute amicably through discussion between their authorised representatives. If the dispute is not resolved within 30 days, it shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts at Bengaluru. This MOU is governed by the laws of India.

17.General

This MOU, together with the training requirement accepted on the portal, is the entire understanding between the Parties on this programme. Any change shall be valid only if made in writing and accepted by both Parties. Notices may be sent by email to the addresses above or through the portal.
