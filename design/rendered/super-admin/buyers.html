How buyer accounts are made

  • Order confirmed
    An admin confirms the UPI payment in Buy › Orders.
  • Account created
    A buyer login is made with the order’s name and email, and a set-password link is emailed.
  • Books delivered
    The books are emailed and also appear in the buyer’s “My books”.
