MOU

Your trainer MOUs — one for each college and batch you train.

How it works

  1. You get itSent when you are scheduled for your first session with a new batch — once per batch, not every session.
  2. Read and signDownload the PDF, check the payment terms, sign every page and the last page.
  3. Upload and shareUpload the signed copy and press Share signed MOU. The admin is notified.
  4. ConfirmedYour sessions for that batch are confirmed. The signed copy stays here.

What every MOU covers

  • Timing
    Reach the college 10 minutes early and the class 5 minutes early; follow the schedule; release students on time.
  • Attendance
    Take it every session and update it on the portal the same day.
  • Dress and equipment
    Formal clothes; carry your own laptop, HDMI cable and adapters.
  • Confidentiality
    Never discuss your pay with other trainers, college staff or students, and don’t share personal contact details with students.
  • Payment
    Per-day rate × days delivered, plus any travel, food or stay; 10% TDS deducted; paid 20–30 days after the training ends.
